## Executive summary

M263은 strict OOF에서 M107과 M244의 완성 예측면을 결합한 첫 독립 계보 앙상블이다. 안정성 선택 결과 그룹 2에만 M244 질량 `0.10`을 적용하고 그룹 1·3은 M107을 유지했다. Q2, Q3, Q4와 pooled Total이 모두 개선됐고 Q4 paired-issuance bootstrap 양수 비율은 `74.9%`로 사전 승격 기준을 통과했다.

## Technical specification

### OOF policy

- Left lineage: `M107_STRICT_TEMPORAL_TOP100`.
- Right lineage: `M244_RARE_EVENT_CORRECTED_ANALOG_Q234`.
- Candidate M244 masses: `0, 0.025, 0.05, 0.075, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25`.
- Selection folds: 2023 Q2 and Q3.
- Eligibility: a nonzero group mass must improve group Total on both selection folds.
- Tie-break: maximize the smaller Q2/Q3 delta, then their mean, then prefer the smaller mass.
- Frozen transfer: apply the selected mass once to Q4.
- Promotion: positive Q2/Q3/Q4 and pooled Total deltas, plus Q4 bootstrap positive fraction above `0.50`.

### Full-history deployment

- Group 1: exact `submission_M261.csv`.
- Group 2: `0.90 * submission_M261 + 0.10 * submission_M252`.
- Group 3: exact `submission_M261.csv`.
- No test-label score, 2024 slice, post-fit selection, scale/offset, dynamic gating, external data, or upload was used.
