## Evidence contract

Every claim below uses **exactly one** manifest-approved tag:

- `directly_supported`: the cited source directly states or demonstrates the exact source-level fact; it does not prove BARAM transfer.
- `contradicts_premise`: direct source evidence cuts against automatic discovery, a unique canonical path, or unconditional low-fidelity safety.
- `near_match_only`: the source operation is adjacent, but its population, evaluator, fidelity, or graph semantics do not satisfy a BARAM evidence gate.
- `insufficient`: the bounded source set does not answer the claim.

A source-level fact is never treated as a local effect estimate. All proposed BARAM mappings are explicitly provisional and require the local evidence listed later.

## Candidate discovery-state machines

### SM-A — minimal append-only falsification machine (**strongest default**)

```text
DEFICIT_IDENTIFIED
  -> RESEARCH_BOUNDED
  -> MIGRATION_HYPOTHESIS_DRAFTED
  -> ADMISSIBILITY_REVIEW
  -> PREDECLARED
  -> F0_DIAGNOSTIC
  -> F1_BOUNDED_SCREEN
  -> F2_STRICT_CONFIRMATION
  -> F3_FIXED_REPRODUCTION
  -> PROMOTED_LOCAL
```

Every arrow appends an event. Result-conditioned exits are typed:

| Observed result | State/edge | Child rule |
|---|---|---|
| Missing scope, chronology, control, falsifier, or authority | `REJECTED_ADMISSIBILITY` | No experiment child; a corrected contract is a new draft. |
| Code/contract/alignment failure before a valid scientific comparison | `EXECUTION_FAILED` → `repair` | Preserve the treatment; repair exactly the defect; do **not** call the hypothesis falsified. |
| Predeclared falsifier observed on a valid surface | `FAILED_FALSIFIER_SCOPED` → `close_scope` | Close the exact treatment/surface/policy tuple; create a different sibling only from a separately justified mechanism. |
| Estimate cannot discriminate, proxy is invalid, or uncertainty gate fails | `INCONCLUSIVE` → `pause` | Record missing evidence and an exact revival predicate; no winner/loser claim. |
| Positive pooled result but component/regime/fold instability | `UNSTABLE` → `stability_child` | Child tests the named instability; it is not a post-hoc promotion. |
| NMAE/FICR or other action-component trade-off invalidates the assumed mechanism | `MECHANISM_CONTRADICTION` → `reenter` | Re-enter metric/action, feature, data, or validation stage with a typed reason. |
| Valid local improvement below global target | `LOCALLY_SUPPORTED` → `residual_deficit` | Preserve the gain and generate the remaining deficit; not `SUCCESS_EXIT`. |
| Exact SK@v5 success contract met | `SUCCESS_EXIT` | Only the skeleton's two deterministic strict reproductions at official `Total >= 0.660000` with full lineage. |
| Feasible portfolio exhausted below target or approval/compute/source blocked | `RESEARCH_PAUSE` | Resumable; never user-goal success. |

**Why defensible:** it imports CRISP-DM re-entry, Adam's test cycle/provenance, AIDE's immutable atomic children, and Co-Scientist's repair-versus-invalidation distinction without importing an unvalidated autonomous ranker. **Cost:** more explicit states and review work; less throughput than greedy score search.

### SM-B — portfolio / critique / evolution machine

```text
SEED_SIBLINGS
  -> INDEPENDENT_ADMISSIBILITY_CRITIQUE
  -> SIMILARITY_CLUSTER_AND_DEDUP
  -> PRIORITY_OR_PAIRWISE_TRIAGE
  -> ROOT_SELECTS_EXPERIMENT
  -> OBJECTIVE_RESULT_UPDATE
  -> {EVOLVE_NEW_CHILD | DIVERGENT_SIBLING | CLOSE_SCOPE | REENTER | PAUSE}
  -> FAMILY_META_REVIEW
```

- The proximity graph is for duplicate control and navigation, **not** evidence of efficacy.
- Pairwise/Elo-style comparison may prioritize literature plausibility, testability, causal leverage, or cost; it may not promote a model.
- Evolution always creates a new node and competes against the retained parent.
- Meta-review records recurring failure mechanisms and changes future admissibility prompts/rules.

**Trade-off:** broader hypothesis coverage and explicit diversity, but subjective shared-critic error, positional bias, high review cost, and novelty theatre. S10's own limitations make a full tournament unjustified as the default.

### SM-C — calibrated multi-fidelity scheduler

```text
ELIGIBLE
  -> F0 contract/cheap mechanism diagnostic
  -> F1 small bounded screen
  -> F2 full strict prequential adjudication
  -> F3 fixed-policy reproduction
```

At each fidelity: `PROMOTE`, `PARK_LOW_FIDELITY`, `CLOSE_ONLY_IF_FULL_FALSIFIER`, or `REPAIR`. Use several predeclared brackets or an exploration reserve; always retain full-fidelity anchors. **Hard gate:** before activation, an archived family-stratified audit must show that the lower fidelity preserves the decision relevant at the next fidelity. A low-fidelity loss alone never closes a family.

**Trade-off:** can save compute when fidelities are predictive; can systematically discard winners when fold count, time span, policy, or resource changes rankings. C08 makes this option conditional, not a default.

### SM-D — stage-maturity and re-entry machine

```text
UNMAPPED
 -> FOUNDATION_MAPPED
 -> RESEARCH_COVERED
 -> MIGRATION_READY
 -> EXPERIMENTED
 -> ADJUDICATED
 -> RESIDUALIZED_SNAPSHOT
       ↘ REENTERED(reason, target_stage) ↗
```

`RESIDUALIZED_SNAPSHOT` means “adequately characterized at this event sequence,” not permanently complete. A new contradiction, material residual, eligible method family, corrected authority assumption, or invalidated validation surface can create `REENTERED`. This is the workflow-level counterpart to SM-A; it prevents binary ontology coverage from masquerading as depth.

Round-robin by stage/cluster plus aging | S10 proximity/diversity; S05 exploration reserve | Prevents one family or greedy incumbent from monopolizing work. | Can spend budget on weak ideas; cluster definition may be subjective. | Useful portfolio safeguard with a small fixed exploration slot. |
| A3. Fixed exploration/exploitation mixture | S05 random fraction | Simple protection against surrogate lock-in. | Exact fraction has no BARAM evidence and must count as a choice. | Option after budget freeze; no literature-derived magic fraction. |
| A4. Hyperband-style brackets | S04 | Robustly spans breadth/depth and aggressive/conservative allocation when fidelity is meaningful. | Temporal/data/policy subsamples may reorder candidates; overhead can dominate small tasks. | **Blocked pending fidelity audit.** |
| A5. BOHB-style learned acquisition | S05 | Learns from prior comparable results while retaining exploration and highest-fidelity preference. | Too few observations, mixed node families, nonstationarity, and repeated-fold adaptation can make the density model spurious. | Later option only after enough commensurate observations and validation-lane approval. |
| A6. Freeze-thaw information gain | S03 | Makes pause/revive explicit and values uncertainty reduction rather than only current score. | Exponential/asymptotic curve assumption and comparable partial observations are unverified for workflow nodes. | Rule-based pause/revival now; probabilistic acquisition blocked. |
| A7. Pairwise tournament / Elo triage | S10 | Can compare heterogeneous prose hypotheses and expose critiques. | Subjective, self-referential, order/critic bias; no objective-ground-truth guarantee. | Ideation triage only; never experiment promotion. |
| A8. Greedy best-score child | S09's simple policy | Minimal overhead and directly result-conditioned. | Scalar repeated-surface overfit, local search, diversity collapse, no falsification distinction. | **Reject as an unmodified DS default.** Borrow only lineage and atomicity. |

## Falsification, stopping, closure, and revival semantics

### Minimum hypothesis/falsifier contract

A node is experiment-admissible only when it freezes:

1. `claim_scope`: population/time/horizon/group/regime and applicability boundary;
2. `local_deficit` and exact diagnostic lineage;
3. `mechanism` and the expected causal/effect channel;
4. exact `treatment`, `control`, row/alignment keys, fit/selection surface, and policy;
5. primary outcome plus component/stability outcomes and a direction/minimum-effect rule supplied later by DS/validation design;
6. `falsifier`: what valid result would reject the mechanism **at this scope**;
7. `inconclusive_conditions`: invalid execution, proxy failure, insufficient discrimination, or contract breach;
8. DOF/candidate family/trial/compute budget and multiplicity identity;
9. child rule for supported, contradicted, unstable, execution-failed, or inconclusive outcomes.

This is stricter than DiscoveryBench's binary `supported/unsupported` and AIDE's scalar score. It is a **provisional BARAM governance hypothesis**, not a standard proven by S01–S10.

### Status meanings

| Status | Meaning | May it revive? |
|---|---|---|
| `REJECTED_ADMISSIBILITY` | Contract or authority never permitted a scientific test. | Yes, only as a corrected new draft. |
| `EXECUTION_FAILED` | Implementation/contract failed; mechanism was not tested. | Yes, via bounded `repair` child. |
| `FAILED_FALSIFIER_SCOPED` | A valid predeclared result falsified the exact scoped treatment. | Original event never changes. A new child requires genuinely new evidence, treatment, or scope—not relabeling. |
| `INCONCLUSIVE` | Valid evidence was not discriminating or fidelity was invalid. | Yes, when the named missing information becomes available. |
| `PAUSED_APPROVAL`, `PAUSED_RESOURCE`, `PAUSED_SOURCE` | Feasible work is blocked, not disproven. | Yes, on the exact approval/resource/source predicate. |
| `SUPERSEDED` | A descendant is preferred, while the parent remains reproducible. | Parent is not reopened; it may still be a control. |
| `LOCALLY_SUPPORTED` | One predeclared local comparison cleared its stated gate. | It can spawn confirmation/residual children; it is not global success. |
| `PROMOTED_LOCAL` | Clears all DS-defined local promotion gates and reproduction. | Can be demoted by a later contradiction with an event; history remains. |
| `RESEARCH_PAUSE` | Global work is resumably blocked/exhausted below target. | Yes; never equivalent to `SUCCESS_EXIT`. |

### Closure scope

Never close “the family” from one node. A closure key should include at least:

```text
(mechanism, treatment, data_snapshot, chronology/fit_surface,
 validation_surface, policy, metric/components, alignment_keys, budget)
```

A closure receipt states what was tested, what was **not** tested, residual uncertainty, viable siblings, and its revival predicates. This directly prevents the old “explicit unknown → stage complete → exhausted below target” failure.

### Legitimate revival predicates

A paused/closed scope may generate a **new descendant** when one of these is recorded before looking at a rescued result:

- a new directly supported local diagnostic or qualifying primary source changes the mechanism;
- an execution/chronology/alignment defect is identified and isolated;
- a previously invalid low fidelity becomes calibrated against full adjudication;
- approval, compute, licence, or source access named in the pause receipt becomes available;
- a later promoted pipeline materially changes the residual population or control, so the old treatment is a genuinely different comparison;
- a contradiction forces upstream re-entry and yields a new foundation snapshot.

“Try again because the result was disappointing,” a new post-hoc slice, or silently altered policy/row keys is not revival; it is uncounted adaptive rescue.

### Node stop versus global stop

- **Node stop:** admissibility rejection; scoped falsifier; bounded repair cap; dominance only on a commensurate predeclared surface; or node budget exhaustion. It does not close siblings/upstream stages.
- **Family pause:** all frozen siblings are closed/paused and a family meta-review names what evidence could reseed it.
- **Global pause:** authority/source/compute ceiling or bounded evidence exhaustion, with residual deficit and resume conditions.
- **Global success:** only the exact SK@v5 `SUCCESS_EXIT`; no source in this lane alters it.

## Provisional workflow maturity rubric

This rubric is synthesized from C01–C04, C13, and C15; the exact levels are **not** a published standard and require root review.

| Level | Required evidence | What it does **not** mean |
|---|---|---|
| M0 `INVENTORIED` | Stage/subcapability has a name and owner. | Coverage, research, or readiness. |
| M1 `FOUNDATION` | Purpose/data/metric/chronology contract and exact local deficit artifact. | A method has been found. |
| M2 `RESEARCHED` | Bounded primary-source ledger, evidence tags, scope gaps, and rejected alternatives. | A near match transfers. |
| M3 `MIGRATION_READY` | Frozen mapping, control, DOF/budget, falsifier, fidelity and availability checks. | Permission to fit; separate approvals still govern. |
| M4 `EXPERIMENTED` | Root-owned valid execution with full lineage and component results. | Promotion or generalization. |
| M5 `ADJUDICATED` | Stability/multiplicity/appropriateness decision and result-conditioned child. | Independent holdout evidence where none exists. |
| M6 `REPRODUCED_RESIDUALIZED` | Fixed-policy reproduction or scoped closure, residual deficit, and revival predicate. | Global success below 0.660000. |

A stage snapshot should report the distribution of material subcapabilities over M0–M6, not a Boolean. Exact aggregation/thresholds remain unknown; a single M6 path must not hide M0/M1 material deficits.

## Failure modes and defenses

| Failure mode | Why it is material | Defense |
|---|---|---|
| Modular “SOTA” guarantee | C12/C16/C20 show sophisticated systems remain unreliable; source benchmark gains do not transfer. | Treat literature as hypothesis generation; require local controlled evidence. |
| Conflating graph kinds | Semantic, transform, solution, and experiment trees encode different relations. | Typed graph layer and edge ontology; explicit cross-layer links. |
| Mutable parent / lost negative result | Erases provenance and encourages post-hoc narratives. | Append-only child; parent/result hashes immutable. |
| Multi-change child | Effect cannot be attributed and hidden DOF grow. | One material delta or enumerate/count all changes. |
| Execution failure called falsification | Tests code, not mechanism. | Separate `EXECUTION_FAILED` and repair edge. |
| Cheap fidelity rank reversal | C08: resource-dependent settings can make small-resource results non-indicative. | Local concordance/false-prune audit; full anchors; park rather than close. |
| Greedy score tree on reused folds | AIDE's stateless scalar assumption does not match adaptive dependent validation. | Vector adjudication and validation-lane multiplicity contract; exploration reserve. |
| Tournament self-confirmation | Elo/critic preference is subjective and may share model error. | Triage only; objective root-owned experiment decides. |
| Diversity collapse / duplicate branches | Best-node refinement repeatedly searches one basin. | Similarity clusters, duplicate cap, divergent sibling slot. |
| Novelty theatre / hallucinated rationale | Co-Scientist acknowledges factuality and homogenization risks. | Primary-source provenance, critic separate from generator, no novelty promotion bonus. |
| Binary maturity | An ontology name or executable script can conceal missing decisions (C14–C15). | Maturity vector and residualization audit. |
| Permanent family closure from one treatment | Scope, policy, fit surface, and control matter. | Scoped closure key and explicit untested siblings. |
| Unprincipled revival | Repeated rescue inflates adaptivity and rewrites hypotheses. | Frozen revival predicate; new descendant; multiplicity identity. |
| Global exhausted halt below target | Confuses budget/evidence pause with requested outcome. | `RESEARCH_PAUSE`; only SK success contract exits. |
| Unbounded recursion / compute leak | Result-conditioned children can grow forever. | Per-node repair depth, family trial budget, stage budget, and portfolio meta-review. |
| Score-only maturity | Final Total can hide component/regime instability or policy/alignment error. | Exact Total plus components/stability/lineage; appropriateness decision. |

## Concrete DS@v5 options and trade-offs

### DS-O1 — deterministic scientific lineage core

Add explicit node kinds (`deficit`, `research_claim`, `migration_hypothesis`, `experiment`, `adjudication`, `residual_deficit`) and typed immutable edges (`seed`, `repair`, `refine`, `branch`, `replicate`, `reenter`, `supersede`, `revive`). Implement SM-A and separate graph layers.

- **Pros:** best direct support; small-state, auditable, backwards-compatible in principle; no learned scheduler.
- **Cons:** schema/test work and manual prioritization; does not itself improve a forecast.
- **Evidence posture:** **recommended foundation** for DS@v5.

### DS-O2 — portfolio-lite overlay

On eligible M3 nodes, add similarity clusters, duplicate control, a fixed exploration slot, and a family meta-review. Perform generation/critique/ranking as logical root roles; no autonomous multi-agent requirement.

- **Pros:** limits tunnel vision and captures recurring failure information.
- **Cons:** similarity and priority rules are new DOF; review overhead; subjective triage.
- **Evidence posture:** defensible optional overlay if every priority decision is logged and cannot promote.

### DS-O3 — calibrated fidelity ladder

Define F0 diagnostic, F1 bounded screen, F2 full strict adjudication, F3 reproduction; then use conservative/exploratory brackets and full anchors.

- **Pros:** potential compute savings and more breadth.
- **Cons:** highest risk of false pruning on a small nonstationary time series; calibration itself consumes trials; method families may have different proxy behavior.
- **Evidence posture:** **blocked** until family-stratified local fidelity evidence and validation-lane approval exist.

### DS-O4 — learned BOHB/freeze-thaw acquisition

Fit a surrogate or information-gain policy to decide new, paused, or resumed nodes.

- **Pros:** theoretically attractive use of accumulated observations.
- **Cons:** heterogeneous nodes are not a clean numeric configuration space; too few comparable high-fidelity observations; adaptive validation leakage/multiplicity risk; surrogate uncertainty may be fiction.
- **Evidence posture:** research-only future option, not current DS@v5 default.

### DS-O5 — full co-scientist-style tournament

Use generator/reflection/ranking/evolution/proximity/meta-review automation and pairwise rankings.

- **Pros:** rich structured ideation and critique.
- **Cons:** high compute/tool dependency, subjective Elo, shared hallucination, preliminary biomedical evidence, and no dependent-TS promotion validity.
- **Evidence posture:** **do not adopt as the execution engine**. Only its logical role separation, immutable evolution, and proximity/meta-review patterns should migrate through DS-O2.

### DS-O6 — maturity and scoped-closure layer

Attach M0–M6 facets, closure key, residual deficit, and revival predicate to each stage/node; convert below-target exhaustion to `RESEARCH_PAUSE`.

- **Pros:** directly repairs the skeleton's binary completion and false terminal stop defects.
- **Cons:** exact stage aggregation and material-subcapability threshold need design and tests.
- **Evidence posture:** **recommended cross-cutting invariant**, with thresholds explicitly provisional.

### Smallest defensible package

`DS-O1 + DS-O6`, with the diversity pieces of `DS-O2`, is the strongest evidence-aligned package. `DS-O3` must remain feature-flagged/off until audited; `DS-O4` and the full `DS-O5` are not justified on the present local evidence. This is a workflow conclusion only, not a BARAM model-family recommendation.

## Contradictions that DS@v5 should preserve

1. **There can be multiple defensible paths, not one canonical SOTA pipeline** (C13), yet BARAM promotion still needs one exact reproducible treatment. Store siblings; do not average their evidentiary status.
2. **Breadth helps discovery, but adaptation consumes validity.** AIDE/Co-Scientist reward more iterative search, while DiscoveryBench/ScienceAgentBench show automation remains unreliable and the dependent-validation question is unanswered (C12, C16, C18, C21).
3. **Early stopping saves compute only when fidelity is informative.** Hyperband is a benchmark practice and also supplies its own warning that resource changes can destroy informativeness (C07–C08).
4. **Pause/revival is operationally valuable but statistically unproven for heterogeneous nodes.** Freeze-Thaw directly supports retained partial state but only under a learning-curve model (C05–C06).
5. **Critique/ranking improves triage, not truth.** Co-Scientist's rankings are paired with real experiments, while its expert preferences remain subjective and validation preliminary (C19–C20).
6. **A running artifact is not a mature scientific result.** ScienceAgentBench separates execution, task success, rubric stages, and cost; BLADE separately measures decision coverage (C14–C15).

## Unknowns / evidence still needed

1. **Fidelity:** no measured mapping from any proposed BARAM F0/F1 proxy to F2 strict ranking, component effects, or false-prune rate (`C08`, `C21`).
2. **Adaptive validity:** no source here establishes a safe acquisition rule on the repeatedly reused dependent 2022–2023 surface with no fresh independent holdout (`C18`, `C21`). The validation lane must govern this.
3. **Graph benefit:** no evidence that an explicit discovery graph itself changes forecast performance versus improving auditability and search discipline (`C22`).
4. **Portfolio size/budgets:** sibling count, repair depth, exploration reserve, similarity threshold, and family/stage trial caps are analyst choices with no BARAM calibration.
5. **Maturity aggregation:** the M0–M6 vector is defensible as a rubric, but the threshold for a stage snapshot to advance versus deepen is unresolved.
6. **Closure granularity:** the proposed closure tuple is conservative; whether mechanism-level closure is ever warranted requires multiple commensurate nodes and root adjudication.
7. **Revival threshold:** no objective value-of-information threshold is supported; approval/evidence/defect predicates are safer than a fitted revival score today.
8. **Pairwise ranking value:** when an exact local metric eventually exists, it is unknown whether Elo-style prose ranking adds enough triage value to justify its bias and cost.
9. **Role implementation:** logical separation is supported; whether separate reviewers/prompts/processes improve root decisions locally is untested.
10. **Source cutoff interpretation for S10:** S10 was published online on 2026-05-19 and therefore meets the cutoff; its volume issue date is 2026-07-09. If the root elects an issue-date-only rule, downgrade S10-dependent migrations to hypotheses. DS-O1/DS-O6 remain supported by S01–S09.
11. **Performance:** nothing in this lane estimates a score gain or closes any preprocessing, feature, model, or validation family (`C22`).

## DS@v5 implications in one page

1. Make the graph **scientific and append-only**, not merely a task queue: explicit hypothesis, test, falsifier, result vector, interpretation, residual, and child rule.
2. Separate `stage_depth` from `node_depth`, and separate stage/deficit, hypothesis/experiment, and data/transform graphs.
3. Never mutate a parent. Create typed atomic children; distinguish `repair` from `refine`, `replicate`, `reenter`, and `revive`.
4. Replace binary completion with a maturity vector and a result-conditioned appropriateness decision.
5. Close an exact scope, not an entire family; store what remains untested and a frozen revival predicate.
6. Preserve diversity with clustering/de-duplication and a small logged exploration reserve; do not let a tournament or LLM approve itself.
7. Treat multi-fidelity as an empirical hypothesis. No proxy-based permanent pruning until rank/recall/false-prune behavior is locally audited by family.
8. Keep `RESEARCH_PAUSE` distinct from `SUCCESS_EXIT`; only the approved SK@v5 reproduction/score contract ends the loop.
9. Treat every literature migration as provisional and every local promotion as a separate root-owned evidence layer.

**Final lane verdict:** the workflow architecture defect identified by SK@v5 is real and repairable using well-supported scientific-workflow mechanics. The defensible migration is deliberately smaller than the most autonomous systems reviewed: immutable structured lineage, explicit falsification and interpretation, bounded result-conditioned children, maturity/residualization, and scoped pause/closure/revival. Automatic SOTA discovery, greedy scalar tree search, subjective tournament promotion, and uncalibrated multi-fidelity pruning are not supported for this dependent time-series setting.
